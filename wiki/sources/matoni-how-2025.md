## Summary
The digital transformation of our society is a constant challenge, as data is generated in almost every digital interaction. To use data effectively, it must be of high quality. This raises the question: what exactly is data quality? A systematic literature review of the existing literature shows that data quality is a multifaceted concept, characterized by a number of quality dimensions. However, the definitions of data quality vary widely. We used feature-oriented domain analysis to specify a taxonomy of data quality definitions and to classify the existing definitions.
