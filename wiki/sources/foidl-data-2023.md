## Summary
Data pipelines are an integral part of various modern data-driven systems. However, despite their importance, they are often unreliable and deliver poor-quality data. A critical step toward improving this situation is a solid understanding of the aspects contributing to the quality of data pipelines. Therefore, this article first introduces a taxonomy of 41 factors that influence the ability of data pipelines to provide quality data.
