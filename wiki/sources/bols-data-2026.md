## Summary
The use of ratio variables in convex Data Envelopment Analysis (DEA) models has long been recognized as a methodological issue, as ratios may be incompatible with the convexity assumptions underlying standard DEA. However, in this paper, we prove that if all variables are ratio variables sharing a common denominator, then convex combinations of feasible activities are also feasible.
