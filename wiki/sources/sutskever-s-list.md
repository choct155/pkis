## Summary
Book node and collection hub — Richard Heimann, *Sutskever's List: Foundational Ideas of Modern AI* (Manning, 2026). Reconstructs the ~30-item reading list Ilya Sutskever reportedly gave John Carmack as a narrative arc, one chapter per cluster of papers. The book's chapter front matter names 27 items; Ch. 1 (biography/OpenAI context) has none, and the epilogue ("The missing pieces") covers what the list omits — RL, multimodal, post-2020 work.
